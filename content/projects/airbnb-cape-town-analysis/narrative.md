# Airbnb Market Analysis - Cape Town

## 📌 Overview

This project provides a comprehensive **investment analysis** of Airbnb properties in Cape Town, South Africa. Using data analytics and market research methodologies, we identified key opportunities for property investment in the short-term rental market.

### Key Objectives

- Identify the most profitable property types
- Map the best locations for investment
- Analyze revenue patterns from successful hosts
- Provide actionable investment recommendations

## 🔍 Business Challenge

The Cape Town Airbnb market presents unique opportunities and challenges for property investors. With the city's growing tourism industry and diverse neighborhoods, understanding where and what to invest in requires careful data analysis.

### Research Questions

- Which property types generate the highest returns?
- What locations command premium pricing?
- How do top hosts optimize their listings?
- What investment strategy maximizes ROI?

## 📊 Methodology

### Data Collection

We analyzed comprehensive Airbnb listing data for Cape Town, including:

- Property types and configurations
- Pricing across different neighborhoods
- Host performance metrics
- Guest ratings and reviews
- Occupancy patterns

### Analysis Approach

1. **Property Type Performance**: Evaluated revenue potential across apartments, houses, guest suites, and unique properties
2. **Geographic Analysis**: Mapped pricing and demand across Cape Town neighborhoods
3. **Host Strategy**: Identified patterns among top-performing hosts
4. **Financial Modeling**: Projected ROI for different investment scenarios

## 🏆 Key Findings

### Top-Performing Property Types

- **Tiny Houses**: Highest guest ratings and unique appeal
- **Guest Suites**: Strong balance of investment cost vs. returns
- **Modern Villas**: Premium pricing opportunities with ocean/mountain views

### Revenue Insights

- Top hosts generate **over ZAR 57 million annually**
- Properties with Table Mountain views command 40% premium
- Waterfront locations show consistent high occupancy

### Investment Recommendation

**Optimal Investment Profile**:
- Property Type: Modern villa or luxury apartment
- Location: Atlantic Seaboard or City Bowl
- Features: Table Mountain or ocean views
- Configuration: 2-3 bedrooms with premium amenities

**Projected Returns**:
- Estimated annual revenue: ZAR 800K - 1.2M
- Target occupancy rate: 75-85%
- Break-even timeline: 5-7 years
